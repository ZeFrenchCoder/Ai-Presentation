# Vectorizing CVs for Technical Mandates

**ID:** HR-01  
**Platform:** Azure AI Search + ChatGPT  

## Target Audience
HR / Tech Recruitment

## AI Used
ChatGPT/MS Copilot

## AI Knowledge Level
Advanced

## Technical Summary
Create a vector database of CVs searchable by profile, skill or project context to optimize the technical recruitment process.

## Content

### Implementation Steps

1. **Secure CV Upload**
   - Upload your CV to ChatGPT
   - **Important Security:** Ensure using Microsoft's MS Copilot for corporate CVs

2. **CV Interrogation with AI**
   Once the CV is uploaded, you can ask various questions to optimize your application.

### Query Examples by Category

#### **Summary & positioning**
- "Summarize my profile in 5 lines for an IT recruiter."
- "What is my 30-second pitch, professional, clear and concrete tone?"

#### **Strengths & gap identification**
- "What are my 5 distinctive strengths, with supporting evidence?"
- "Identify time gaps or inconsistencies and suggest neutral ways to explain them."

#### **ATS system optimization**
- "List the implicit technical keywords from my CV and those missing for a [job title] position."

#### **Achievement transformation**
- "Transform my tasks into quantified achievements (STAR method)."

#### **Specific job offer targeting**
- "Here is the job description (pasted below). Compare it to my CV:
  - Major gaps
  - Missing keywords
  - Reformulation suggestions"

#### **Redesign and alternative formats**
- "Rewrite the Experience section with impact-oriented bullets (max 2 lines/bullet)."
- "Generate a LinkedIn profile version (≈2,600 characters) consistent with my CV."
- "Export my data in JSON (experiences, skills, studies) for reuse."

## Summary

This method allows HR and technical recruitment teams to optimize CV analysis and presentation through artificial intelligence. By vectorizing CVs in a searchable database, recruiters can quickly identify candidates matching the sought profiles, improve the quality of candidate presentations, and customize CVs according to specific offers. The approach combines security (via MS Copilot) and efficiency to transform the technical recruitment process into a more intelligent and precise system.
